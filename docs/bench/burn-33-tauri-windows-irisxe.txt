Reading it:
- Same failures as in Edge: every 5 µm case fails with "framebuffer incomplete: 0x8cdd"
  (GL_FRAMEBUFFER_UNSUPPORTED) after the grid is planned as 3 × 3 tiles padded to ≤ 6711², and the
  WebGL context is lost after it. The live case fails at its first spike-job refresh even with a fresh
  context (gl error before: 0 in the rerun).
- The spike job passes on the median (warm full-res 1396.3 ms, scrub 16.3 ms), but warm full-res
  varies widely (542.2 to 2748.7 ms) and its max is over 2 s. That is about 12× the M1.
  With thermal, warm full-res is 361.9 ms and the thermal step 1.81 s.
- Measured only; no code changed.
