Reading it:
- The 5 µm pitch only just meets the 2 s full-res budget on an M1 in the real webview (1.83 s warm),
  and misses it on first render. Iris Xe will be slower, so it will very likely miss there.
  The blur passes are most of it (stage-synced: blurH 283 ms + blurV 443 ms of ~970 ms).
- The hidden Chromium pane overstated the thermal step about 3× on the spike job. Use these numbers,
  not the hidden-pane ones, as the macOS baseline.
- Live refreshes on thermal materials still grow with the job (0.6 → 0.8 s spike, 0.6 → 1.8 s at
  5 µm), so the app's refresh interval reaches ~1.6–3.5 s. See BURN-35.
- GL error 0 throughout. Conservation and GPU-vs-CPU checks pass.
